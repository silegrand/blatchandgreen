What We Do

Complete Delivery,
From Ground to Finish

Blatch & Green provides the full spectrum of luxury construction and project delivery — from initial site appraisal through to final snagging and handover.

Most firms specialise in a part of the build. Blatch & Green is responsible for all of it.

From the first spade in the ground to the final coat of paint, every discipline is managed, warranted and delivered by a single team. No handoffs. No diluted accountability. Whether the commission is a ground-up new build, a listed restoration or a complete interior fit-out, the standard applied is the same — and the people responsible for it never change.

01

Principal Contractor

Full responsibility for the design, programme, budget and delivery of your project under a JCT or bespoke contract. We become your single point of accountability, engaging and warranting every specialist on site.

What this service covers
  • Contract administration and programme management
  • Specialist subcontractor procurement and management
  • Health, safety and CDM compliance
  • Quality assurance at every stage
02

New Build Residential

Ground-up construction of bespoke private residences in London and the South East. From listed-site groundworks through to millimetre-precise interior finishes, working with the finest architects in the country.

What this service covers
  • Structural and civil engineering works
  • Superstructure, envelope and roofing
  • MEP design and installation
  • Bespoke joinery, stone and metalwork
03

Heritage Restoration

Restoration, renovation and conversion of listed buildings, historic country houses and period properties. Our conservation-trained craftsmen work with Historic England and specialist consultants.

What this service covers
  • Listed building consent management
  • Traditional lime render, masonry and leadwork
  • Sensitive structural intervention
  • Period-accurate interior joinery and decoration
04

Interior Fit-Out

Full interior fit-out to the specification of a principal interior designer, or in close collaboration with your design team. Every material sourced, fabricated and installed to the highest available standard.

What this service covers
  • Custom furniture and joinery manufacture
  • Stone and marble procurement and installation
  • Decorative lighting design and installation
  • Smart home and AV integration
