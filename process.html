How We Work

A Process Built on Clarity, Not Convention

Every Blatch & Green commission follows the same careful sequence — refined over forty years into a framework that protects the quality of the outcome at every stage.

01

Initial Conversation

We begin with a private meeting at your home, at our London office, or at the site. No presentations, no brochures. We want to understand your project, your timeline, your priorities, and whether we are the right firm for you.

What this stage produces
  • Mutual understanding of the project scope
  • Preliminary view on programme and budget range
  • Decision on whether to proceed to appraisal
02

Site Appraisal & Cost Plan

We conduct a thorough site visit and prepare a detailed cost plan in collaboration with your architect. We do not provide indicative pricing: every number in our cost plan is a commitment we stand behind.

What this stage produces
  • Detailed elemental cost plan
  • Outline programme with key milestones
  • Risk register and procurement strategy
03

Contract & Mobilisation

We use JCT contracts as a foundation, adapted to the specific requirements of each project. Once signed, we move quickly: site establishment, utility diversions and specialist procurement begin within days.

What this stage produces
  • Executed contract with agreed programme
  • Communication and reporting protocol
  • Named site manager and project director
04

Construction

Weekly progress reports to you. Monthly site walks together. An open-door policy with our site management team. We manage complexity so you do not have to.

What this stage produces
  • Weekly written progress report
  • Photographic record at every stage
  • Monthly cost report against budget
05

Handover & Aftercare

We do not consider a project complete when the keys are handed over. Every commission receives a 12-month aftercare programme — a dedicated contact who responds to any issue within 24 hours, any time.

What this stage produces
  • Complete as-built documentation
  • Warranties and guarantees register
  • 12-month aftercare with named contact
